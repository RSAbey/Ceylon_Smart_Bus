# Deviations from Figma — Member 01 (Accounts)

The supplied Accounts mockups (screens 1–9) were drawn with ride-hailing wording and features. The group decided
to **keep each screen's layout and flow but rewrite the copy for bus travel**, so the app stays consistent with the
Ceylon Smart Bus screens (10–20) and with the agreed ERD. Every change is listed here.

| Screen | Figma | Implemented | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|

| Choose Account (1 of 4) | "Driver / Partner — Register your vehicle, accept bookings, and earn on your schedule" | "Driver / Partner — Run trips, verify passenger tickets, and report delays on your route" | Matches the driver's actual duties in this system |
| Choose Account (1 of 4) | Choosing Driver leads to driver self-registration | Choosing Driver explains that admins create driver accounts, and offers driver sign-in | PROJECT_PLAN.md deviation 4: drivers are registered by an admin, who verifies licence and NIC. ## ERD change made for Member 01

`OTP_VERIFICATION.attemptCount` (number, default 0) was **added** beyond the original ERD.

- **Why:** the Verification screen shows "Remaining attempts: N", and without a counter a 6-digit code could be
  brute-forced in at most a million tries, which breaks NFR-07.
- **Action needed:** update `docs/ERD_AND_RELATIONAL.md` §3 and the relational notation, and regenerate the
  EER/relational diagrams in `docs/diagrams/` before the report is submitted.

## Known limitation to confirm with the group

The ERD has no "verified" flag on `USER` (`status` is only `active | blocked`). An account is therefore created
and usable as soon as registration succeeds; the OTP gates the registration flow but does not block a later
direct sign-in. Options: accept and document it, or add `isMobileVerified` to `USER` and enforce it at login.
